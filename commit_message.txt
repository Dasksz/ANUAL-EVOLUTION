fix: enable filtering for Salty and Foods on Por Filial slide

The "Salty" and "Foods" buttons on the "Por Filial" slide in the closing presentation were unresponsive.

This commit introduces event listeners for the `#presentation-filial-filters button` elements within the `setupFilters()` initialization logic. When clicked, these buttons update the `currentFilialFilter` state, toggle the active button styling, and dispatch a `change` event on the filial select element to force a re-render of the specific filial data.
